### What each one measures, and why it matters to an employer

**Practical AI Fluency**
- Engine name: AI Fluency
- Also answers to: AI Fluency (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Business AI Fluency (business)
- What it measures: Whether your people know from use where a tool is reliable and where it is confidently wrong.
- Business meaning: This is whether AI is producing real leverage in your organisation or only activity. Fluency is learned from use, so it responds quickly to practice.
- Strength: Your people know from experience where these tools help and where they fail.
- Developing: Use is real but shallow, and the failure modes are not yet familiar.
- Watch: There is little practical experience to judge the tools by.
- Related composites: futureReadiness, augmentation, underexposure
**Decision Ownership**
- Engine name: Human Agency
- Also answers to: Human Agency (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Owner Decision Ownership (business)
- Exposure reported as: delegated decisions
- What it measures: Whether your people stay the author of the work and the owner of the decision when AI is in the room.
- Business meaning: When a decision is questioned later, someone in your organisation can explain why it was made. Where this is weak, accountability moves to a tool that cannot carry it.
- Strength: Your people hold the decision and can account for it.
- Developing: Ownership is real but uneven, and it thins under time pressure.
- Watch: Decisions are moving to the tool faster than the responsibility for them is.
- Related composites: judgment, augmentation, dependencyIndex
**Better Thinking, Not Only Faster**
- Engine name: Cognitive Amplification
- Also answers to: Cognitive Amplification (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Strategic Amplification (business)
- What it measures: Whether AI is widening how your people think, or only speeding up what they produce.
- Business meaning: This separates a faster organisation from a better one. Speed without better thinking compounds mistakes as readily as it compounds work.
- Strength: AI is improving the quality of thinking, not only the speed of output.
- Developing: The gains are mostly in speed, with some improvement in quality.
- Watch: AI is producing more work rather than better work.
- Related composites: augmentation, futureReadiness
**Independent Capability**
- Engine name: Independent Capability
- Also answers to: Dependency Risk (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Operational Continuity (business); Continuity Risk (business); Unaided Capability (professional); Reliance Risk (professional)
- What it measures: Whether your people could still do the work in the week the tool is unavailable.
- Business meaning: This is your continuity reading. It tells you how much of your output would survive a tool outage, a price change or a vendor disappearing.
- Strength: The work would still get done without the tool.
- Developing: Some of the work would be slower or harder to reproduce unaided.
- Watch: A meaningful share of the output would be difficult to reproduce without the tool.
- Related composites: capabilityTransfer, dependencyIndex
**Checking Before You Act**
- Engine name: Verification & Judgment
- Also answers to: Verification & Judgment (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Verification Before Consequence (business)
- Exposure reported as: unverified claims
- What it measures: Whether AI-supplied claims get checked somewhere the tool did not supply, in proportion to what being wrong would cost.
- Business meaning: This is the capability standing between a confident wrong answer and a customer, a contract or your accounts.
- Strength: Checking is routine and scaled to what is at stake.
- Developing: Some checking happens, but not yet at the level mature AI use needs.
- Watch: Confident output is being taken at face value.
- Related composites: judgment, futureReadiness
**Craft That Still Grows**
- Engine name: Skill Growth
- Also answers to: Skill Growth (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Team Capability Growth (business)
- What it measures: Whether the underlying craft keeps developing while AI carries more of the output.
- Business meaning: This is your bench. Where it thins, you lose the people who can tell whether AI output is any good.
- Strength: The underlying craft is still developing alongside AI.
- Developing: Skills are holding rather than growing.
- Watch: The foundational capability is thinning behind the assisted output.
- Related composites: capabilityTransfer
**Your Own Voice**
- Engine name: Creative Leverage
- Also answers to: Creative Leverage (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Market Differentiation (business)
- What it measures: Whether what your people produce still sounds like your organisation.
- Business meaning: This is whether your work still sounds like you. Where it is weak, your organisation produces what every competitor’s AI produces.
- Strength: The work keeps a distinct voice.
- Developing: Some of the output is distinctive and some of it is generic.
- Watch: The output is drifting towards what any tool would produce.
- Related composites: none
**The Line You Hold**
- Engine name: Responsible Use
- Also answers to: Responsible Use (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Governance, Data, and Trust (business)
- Exposure reported as: confidential information
- What it measures: Whether the line about other people’s information holds when holding it is inconvenient.
- Business meaning: This is your exposure on confidential information and on honest dealing. It is the dimension a regulator, a client or a journalist would ask about.
- Strength: The boundary on confidential information and honest use holds.
- Developing: The boundary is understood but not consistently applied.
- Watch: Confidential information and disclosure are being handled case by case.
- Related composites: judgment
**What You Keep**
- Engine name: Learning Transfer
- Also answers to: Learning Transfer (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Institutional Knowledge Capture (business)
- What it measures: Whether work done with help becomes capability your people keep.
- Business meaning: This is whether you are buying output or building capability. Where it is weak you are renting the same result again every time.
- Strength: Assisted work is becoming capability your people keep.
- Developing: Some of what is done with help is retained, and some is not.
- Watch: The same help is needed again each time, so nothing is accumulating.
- Related composites: capabilityTransfer, augmentation, futureReadiness
**Deliberate Practice**
- Engine name: Adaptive Growth
- Also answers to: Adaptive Growth (student, teacher, parent, administrator, pastor); Business Adaptability (business)
- What it measures: Whether your people review their own habits on purpose as the tools change.
- Business meaning: Tools change faster than habits do. This reads whether your people revise their practice deliberately or drift with whatever the vendor ships.
- Strength: Habits are reviewed on purpose as the tools change.
- Developing: Practice updates occasionally, usually in reaction to something.
- Watch: Habits formed early are being carried unchanged into different tools.
- Related composites: futureReadiness, underexposure

## The nine profiles

- **The Strategic Integrator**: Deliberate, verified, high-leverage collaboration with AI.
- **The Grounded Selectivist**: Low use by deliberate choice, backed by real judgment.
- **The Augmented Thinker**: AI is measurably improving how you think, not just what you produce.
- **The Capable Traditionalist**: Strong human foundation; the AI-shaped competencies are not yet formed.
- **The Dependent Operator**: Fluent and fast, with the underlying capability thinning.
- **The Uncritical Consumer**: Regular use with the checking switched off.
- **The Curious Explorer**: Real experimentation under way; habits and evaluation still forming.
- **The Hesitant Starter**: Early, uncertain, and largely unformed AI habits.
- **The Forming Practitioner**: Moderate, broadly balanced use; the pattern is forming but not yet distinct.

## What is not collected

Nothing below is estimated, modelled or inferred, and no figure in the report depends on it.

- **AI value**: Task time, quality, rework and throughput against a baseline, so a change in practice could be tied to a change in output.
- **Human capability retention**: Assisted against unaided matched tasks, delayed recall and repeatability, which is the only direct measure of whether capability is being kept.
- **Governance**: Verification coverage, disclosure, sign-off and incidents, turning the exposure readings here into observed rates.
- **Economic value**: Licences, training, time saved, rework and incident cost, which together would give a net task value this report deliberately does not estimate.
- **Organisational conditions**: Training exposure, manager support, safety around experimenting, AI access and policy clarity, which explain why a capability is where it is.
- **Intervention effectiveness**: Baseline, intervention and retake, so movement could be attributed rather than observed.
